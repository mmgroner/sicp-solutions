## What is the value of `sum` after each of the above expressions is evaluated?

After `seq` is evaluated, `sum` is 1. `seq` calls the first element of the stream implicitly, adding 1 to 0.

After `y` is defined, the first even sum of the enumerated intervals is 6.
1 + 2 = 3 is not even. 3 + 3 = 6 which is even. That's the first element of the stream `y`.

When we define `z`, we go even farther into the stream. We find the first sum that is  multiple of 5. 6 + 4 = 10 which is a multiple of 5. `sum` is now 10.

After calling `(stream-ref y 7)`, the sum jumps to 136. We must find the 7th (really the 8th, `stream-ref` is zero-indexed even sum of the stream.

```
y:

6 - index 0

10 - index 1

10 + 5 = 15 !even

15 + 6 = 21 !even

21 + 7 = 28 EVEN - index 2

28 + 8 = 36 EVEN - index 3

36 + 9 = 45 !even

45 + 10 = 55 !even

55 + 11 = 66 EVEN - index 4

66 + 12 = 78 EVEN - index 5

78 + 13 = 91 !even

91 + 14 = 105 !even

105 + 15 = 120 EVEN - index 6

120 + 16 = 136 EVEN - index 7
```

Calling `(display-stream z)` traverses the entirety of the stream, which adds up the complete sum, totaling to 210.

## What are the printed responses to evaluating `stream-ref` and `display-stream`?

In this case, we are not printing anything in the `accum` function, so everything behaves as expected. `stream-ref` returns 136 and prints it to the console. `display-stream` all the elements of the stream on separate lines, which in this case, is all the sums that are multiples of 5 along the way.

## Would these responses differ without memoizing `delay`?

Absolutely. Without memoizing `delay`, everytime we evaluated an expression, the elements that we had already traversed would be added to the sum. So in the beginning, the sum would be 0, then when `seq` is defined, `sum` will be one, then when `y` is defined, `sum` would be 1 + 1 + 2 = 4 (first even sum). Then when `z` is defined, `sum` would be 4 + 1 = 5 (first multiple of 5). The numbers would continue to look very different as evaluation of the program proceeded, leading up to a number that is not the sum of all integers 1 through 20.
